About me

Hi, I’m Ronan. I’m an embedded systems engineer, and I live in Saint-Malo, France, where my sailboat is moored.

I have been taking things apart and making them talk to each other for as long as I can remember. In 2010 I plugged an Arduino into a Minitel, at Supélec I taught an Arduino to speak Logitech, and these days I spend my evenings bringing a Mac SE/30 back to life and designing new cards for it.

This is where I write down what I built and how : mostly embedded systems and retro computers, sometimes film photography, and when the weather is good I’m probably out sailing or hiking.
